# Security

Found a vulnerability? Email security@evotrix.org or security@keirahopkins.co.uk. Don't open a public issue.

Include a description, steps to reproduce, and the impact.

## Patches

Critical issues are fixed and released within a week. You'll get acknowledgment within 48 hours.

## Scope

We maintain security for:
- The served codebase and releases
- Official deployed instances

We don't maintain security for:
- Third party integrations
- Modified versions you deploy
- Dependencies (report upstream)

## Best Practices

- Update to new releases when they come out
- Use strong passwords
- Cloudflare handles TLS
- Restrict access when possible
- Check logs periodically
